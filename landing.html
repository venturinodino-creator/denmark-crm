How it works

Signals,
not spreadsheets.

The platform processes your institution data as live intelligence signals — not static records. Every contact, contract, and competitor product is scored and surfaced automatically.

01
Subscriptions as signals
Every competitor subscription is tracked with status, notes, and confidence level — so you know exactly who has what and when it expires.
02
Contacts stay current
Library directors, research directors, and procurement contacts are stored with priority scores, quality flags, and engagement history.
03
Deals tracked end-to-end
From initial outreach to close, every deal is visible in the pipeline — with institution context always one click away.
